What this page is. A working, single-request crawler: it fetches pages server-side, parses HTML without a browser, and evaluates every check against the real markup and response headers. Site-level checks (robots.txt, sitemap, orphans, duplicate clusters, click depth, exposed files) run once the crawl finishes.

What it is not. A 50,000-page crawler needs a distributed queue, a headless renderer, a datastore and resumable jobs — that architecture, with all 264 checks and their rules, is in the accompanying specification document.
